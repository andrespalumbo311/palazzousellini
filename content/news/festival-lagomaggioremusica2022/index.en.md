Casa Usellini hosts the summer concert series presented by Gioventù Musicale d'Italia. Below is the complete schedule of the 2022 season:

- **28 July (Festival Opening)**:
  **Giuseppe Gibboni**, violin (1st Prize Paganini International Violin Competition Genoa 2021) & **Carlotta Dalia**, guitar.
  *Music by Paganini, Castelnuovo-Tedesco, Piazzolla.*

- **4 August**:
  **Claudia Lucia Lamanna**, harp (1st Prize International Harp Contest in Israel, Tel Aviv 2022).
  *Music by Ravin, Britten, Breschand, Lopez, Bach.*

- **11 August**:
  **Jae Hong Park**, piano (1st Prize Busoni International Piano Competition Bolzano 2021).
  *Music by Schumann, Scriabin, Franck.*

- **18 August**:
  **Chagall Trio**, violin, cello and piano (Trio di Trieste Award 2019).
  *Music by Haydn and Brahms.*
