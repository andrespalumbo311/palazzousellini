On Saturday 24 June, the garden of Palazzo Usellini hosted the press conference and public presentation for the comprehensive Catalogue Raisonné on the painting of Gianfilippo Usellini, edited by art historian Luigi Sansone.

Art historian Elena Pontiggia offered a deeply engaging lecture examining the unique metaphysical and narrative qualities of Gianfilippo Usellini's oeuvre. Conservator Eliana Tovagliaro presented a technical analysis of the master's pictorial methods and restoration discoveries.

Federico Monti, Mayor of Arona, shared personal recollections of the painter—a close friend of his father—and provided an update on the progress of the upcoming Fanny Usellini Municipal Museum, established through the donation of Luigi Sansone's collection. The event concluded with reflections from former students, artists, collectors, friends, and members of the Usellini family.
