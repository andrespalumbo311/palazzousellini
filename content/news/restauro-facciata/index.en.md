During 2023, an extensive conservative restoration was conducted on the exterior facade along Via Pertossi, as well as the east and west elevations of the palace.

The works extended over several months following meticulous stratigraphical examinations executed by the specialist firm SM Munaro Restauri, restoring historic luminosity and philological integrity to the 18th-century exterior.
