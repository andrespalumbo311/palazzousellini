On the occasion of the 29th edition of the LagoMaggioreMusica Festival, Casa Usellini hosts a cycle of classical chamber concerts in the inner garden:

- **Friday 28 July, 21:00**:
  **Simone Gramaglia** (viola) & **Martti Rousi** (cello) in *“Musical Narratives”*.
  *Music by Bach, Mozart, Rolla, Lutoslawski, Paganini.*

- **Friday 4 August, 21:00**:
  **Julian Kainrath** (violin) & **Luigi Carroccia** (piano).
  *Music by Beethoven, Franck.*

- **Friday 11 August, 21:00**:
  **Goldberg String Quartet** (Jingzhi Zhang, Giacomo Lucato, Matilde Simionato, Martino Simionato).
  *Music by Haydn, Beethoven, Verdi.*

- **Friday 18 August, 21:00**:
  **Yukine Kuroki** (piano - 1st Prize Franz Liszt Competition Utrecht 2022).
  *Music by Liszt, Schubert/Liszt.*

> *Note: In case of rain, concerts are relocated to the Church of San Graziano in Arona.*
