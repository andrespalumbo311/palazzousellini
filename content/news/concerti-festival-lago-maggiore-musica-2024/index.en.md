Summer concert programme for the 30th edition of the LagoMaggioreMusica Festival held at Casa Usellini:

- **2 August, 21:00**:
  **Michiaki Ueno** (cello) & **Ani Ter-Martirosyan** (piano).
  *Music by Debussy, Beethoven, Clara Schumann, Brahms.*

- **9 August, 21:00**:
  **Guido Sant’Anna** (violin) & **Martina Consonni** (piano).
  *Music by Brahms, Ravel, Frolov.*

- **16 August, 21:00**:
  **Arsenii Moon** (piano - 1st Prize Busoni Competition 2023).
  *Music by Bach-Busoni, Chopin, Rachmaninoff, Ravel, Debussy, Mussorgsky.*
