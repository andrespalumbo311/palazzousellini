The cultural programme and classical concerts scheduled for the 2025 summer season at Casa Usellini:

- **4 July, 18:00**: *Summer Overture* – Marino Mora & Fabio Pollegioni.
- **25 July, 21:00**: Mariam Abouzahra (violin) & Nora Emödi (piano).
- **1 August, 21:00**: Opus13 String Quartet.
- **8 August, 21:00**: Maria Zaitseva (cello) & Maria Zaitseva Sr (piano).
- **14 August, 21:00**: Dmytro Udovychenko (violin) & Milana Cherniavskaya (piano).
- **22 August, 21:00**: Konstantin Emelyanov (piano).
- **30 & 31 August, 20:30**: *“Usellini, Painter of Dreams”* performed by Arona Città Teatro.
