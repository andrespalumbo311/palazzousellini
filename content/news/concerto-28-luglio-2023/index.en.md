A memorable musical evening at Casa Usellini in collaboration with the LagoMaggioreMusica Festival:

- **Simone Gramaglia**, viola
- **Martti Rousi**, cello

### *“Musical Narratives”*
Performance of chamber compositions by J.S. Bach, W.A. Mozart, A. Rolla, W. Lutoslawski, and N. Paganini in the setting of the inner palace garden.
