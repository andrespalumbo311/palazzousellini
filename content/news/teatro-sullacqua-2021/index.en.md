Casa Usellini hosts several theatrical productions in connection with Arona's annual Teatro Sull'Acqua Festival, staged in the palace garden:

- **Tuesday 7 September, 20:00**: staging of *“Sulla morte senza esagerare”*.
- **Saturday 11 September, 20:00**: theatrical work *“Tiresias”*, with an **encore on Sunday 12 September** at the same time.

For full ticketing and reservations, consult the official [Teatro Sull'Acqua](http://teatrosullacqua.it) platform.
