On **Sunday 27 September 2026 at 17:00**, the garden of **Casa Usellini** in Arona (Vicolo Pertossi) hosts the concert **“End-of-Summer Notes... Great Classical and Pop Music”**.

Performers:
- **Duo Verbano**: Fabio Pollegioni (tenor) and Marino Mora (piano)
- **Special guests**: Daniela Piran and Davide Vella

### Details
- **Date & Time**: Sunday 27 September 2026, 17:00
- **Location**: Casa Usellini, Vicolo Pertossi – Arona (NO)
- **Admission**: Free (voluntary donations welcome)
- **Contact**: Phone +39 342.0057160
- **In case of bad weather**: The performance will take place in the Arona Town Hall Council Hall (Sala Consiliare del Comune di Arona).
