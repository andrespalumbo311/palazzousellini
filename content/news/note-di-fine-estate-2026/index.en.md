In the evocative setting of the garden of **Villa Usellini** in Arona (Vicolo Pertossi), on **Sunday 27 September 2026 at 17:00**, the residence will host the concert **“End-of-Summer Notes... Great Classical and Pop Music”**, organized in collaboration with **Accademia dei Laghi** and under the patronage of the **City of Arona**.

The musical afternoon features the **“Duo Verbano”**:
- **Fabio Pollegioni** – Tenor
- **Marino Mora** – Piano

With the special participation of guest performers:
- **Daniela Piran**
- **Davide Vella**

A captivating musical journey bridging celebrated classical repertory and timeless popular melodies, set in the serene historic atmosphere of Palazzo Usellini.

### Event Details & Useful Information
- **Date & Time**: Sunday 27 September 2026, 17:00
- **Location**: Villa Usellini, Vicolo Pertossi – Arona (NO)
- **Admission**: Free admission – voluntary donations welcome
- **Inquiries**: Phone +39 342.0057160
- **In case of bad weather**: The performance will take place in the Arona Town Hall Council Hall (Sala Consiliare del Comune di Arona).
