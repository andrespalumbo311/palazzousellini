This policy outlines how the official website of **Palazzo Usellini** ([palazzousellini.com](https://palazzousellini.com)) processes visitors' personal data, in full compliance with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 2016/679.

### 1. Data Controller
The data controller is the ownership of Palazzo Usellini:
- **Address**: Via Pertossi 12, 28041 Arona (NO), Italy
- **Contact Email**: [info@palazzousellini.com](mailto:info@palazzousellini.com)

---

### 2. Zero-Cookie Architecture (Privacy by Design)
This website is built with a static site architecture strictly focused on visitor confidentiality:
- **Zero Profiling or Marketing Cookies**: This website does **not** employ any cookies to monitor user behavior or build commercial profiles.
- **Zero Third-Party Trackers**: No invasive tracking pixels or marketing beacons (e.g. Meta Pixel, TikTok tracking, ad networks) are embedded.
- **Self-Hosted Assets**: All typography, scripts, and stylesheets are hosted locally on the server, preventing unnecessary transmission of visitors' IP addresses to external third parties.

Under the ePrivacy Directive and GDPR guidelines, **no intrusive cookie consent banner is required**.

---

### 3. Data Voluntarily Provided by Visitors
Sending an email to the addresses published on this site entails the acquisition of the sender's address to reply to inquiries, alongside any personal details contained in the communication. Such correspondence is never sold to third parties or used for unsolicited commercial marketing.

---

### 4. Visitor Rights
Visitors may exercise their rights at any time under Articles 15+ of EU Regulation 2016/679 (access, rectification, deletion of personal data) by sending an email to [info@palazzousellini.com](mailto:info@palazzousellini.com).
